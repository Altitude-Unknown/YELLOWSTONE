## 2026-07-01 UART correction

- Yellowstone PB22/PB23 are the SHERPA UART pins:
  - PB22 / SAMD21 package pin 37 / Arduino D30 = `Serial5` TX
  - PB23 / SAMD21 package pin 38 / Arduino D31 = `Serial5` RX
- The Feather M0 core's default `Serial1` is PA10/PA11 and does not reach the
  Yellowstone SHERPA UART connector.
- Two-Yellowstone UART diagnostics passed after switching from `Serial1` to
  `Serial5`:
  - `Yellowstone_UART_Ping_Talker`
  - `Yellowstone_UART_Ping_Responder`
- Airborne firmware was updated to forward `SHERPA,CUTDOWN,<sequence>` over
  `Serial5`.

## 2026-07-01 ICARUS acknowledgement path

- Added a reverse acknowledgement for cutdown commands:
  - ICARUS broadcasts an ESP-NOW ack after accepting a new cutdown sequence.
  - SHERPA receives `ICARUS,ACK,<sequence>,accepted,<count>` and forwards it
    to Airborne over the Yellowstone UART.
  - Airborne repeats a compact LoRa ack packet back to Ground.
  - Ground prints `STATUS,ICARUS_ACK,<sequence>,accepted,<count>,rssi,<rssi>`.
  - The GUI displays the ICARUS ack in the Cutdown panel and status bar.
- Ground transmits the cutdown command for about three seconds, so Airborne
  repeats the ack for several seconds to avoid the ack arriving only while
  Ground is still transmitting.
